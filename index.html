The Election Timeline

Follow the steps from registration to results.

1. Voter Registration

Before you can vote, you must register. Check your eligibility and register early. Many states allow online registration, making it easy for students.

2. Campaigning & Debates

Candidates present their platforms. Engage with virtual town halls, watch debates, and research where candidates stand on issues you care about.

3. Early & Absentee Voting

Can't make it home on Election Day? Request a mail-in/absentee ballot to be sent to your college address, or find early voting locations nearby.

4. Election Day

The main event. Head to your assigned polling place, cast your ballot, and ensure your voice is heard!

5. Results & Certification

Votes are counted, results are projected, and eventually officially certified. The peaceful transfer of power begins.

Virtuality & Elections

How the digital world contributes to modern democracy

Social Media Mobilization

Digital platforms allow students to organize rallies, share info rapidly, and mobilize voters in unprecedented ways, driving higher youth turnout.

Virtual Town Halls

Candidates are increasingly using virtual streams like Twitch, YouTube, and Zoom to connect directly with college demographics and answer live Q&A.

Digital Security & E-Voting

Researching secure e-voting systems and protecting election data against misinformation are massive parts of the modern virtual election landscape.
